# Motivation

## Growing closer to home

Many people enjoy growing plants but do not have access to a field or garden. For apartment growers, limited space can make a traditional garden difficult. We became interested in aeroponics as a compact way to grow plants, with nutrient solution delivered as mist to the roots.

{{growing-spaces}}

## Exploring growing methods

Soil-based growing is one approach among several. Hydroponics, aquaponics, and aeroponics offer different ways to manage plants and their growing conditions in constrained spaces. We chose to explore aeroponics by building a controlled testbed for fenugreek.

{{growing-methods}}

## From growing plants to understanding plant health

As we considered growing plants in a controlled system, another challenge became clear: visible symptoms can be difficult to associate with a specific nutrient-solution condition. General solution measurements such as electrical conductivity and pH do not, by themselves, identify visual symptoms on a plant.

We also needed a structured set of plant images over time, connected to the conditions in which the plants were grown. That led us to the connected goals of building a testbed, collecting a time-series image dataset, and developing a model to identify deficiency symptoms associated with the tested fertilizer conditions.

This motivation led to **Intelligent Nutrient Management for Aeroponics**. The project focuses on fenugreek grown under controlled fertilizer conditions. It is intended to support later image-based analysis; the model is a core goal, but model development has not yet begun.
