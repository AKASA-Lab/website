# Methodology

We compare eight nutrient-solution conditions by varying the presence or absence of calcium nitrate, potassium sulphate, and ammonium phosphate. Dataset collection is in progress. This page describes the study design; actual solution concentrations and run-specific records should be documented with the experiment data.

## Study workflow

1. Grow fenugreek in our completed aeroponic testbed.
2. Compare the eight combinations of the three fertilizer inputs.
3. Keep seed source and age, bucket and sprayer configuration, misting schedule, growing environment, solution preparation, and image-capture setup consistent across conditions, and record any deviations.
4. Capture plant images throughout growth using a consistent schedule and viewpoint.
5. Record plant, bucket, treatment, capture-time, growth-day, and observation information with the images.
6. Keep designated test plants and their images separate from training and tuning data for later model evaluation.
7. Begin image analysis and model development after dataset work; model development has not yet started.

## Protocol details to confirm

Our treatment codes identify fertilizer compounds that are present (`1`) or absent (`0`). We do not use them to represent isolated elemental nitrogen, phosphorus, or potassium conditions. Record solution concentrations, treatment allocation, replicate count, environmental measurements, experiment duration, image-capture frequency, camera settings, and observations with each run.
