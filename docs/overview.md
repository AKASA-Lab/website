# Overview

**Intelligent Nutrient Management for Aeroponics** is our project. Smart Aeroponics System is the aeroponic testbed we built for it. We explore aeroponics as a way to grow plants where space is limited, including apartments, and study fenugreek responses to different fertilizer combinations.

Our three main goals are to create the testbed, build a time-series image dataset, and develop a model that can identify deficiency symptoms associated with the fertilizer conditions we test. The model is central to our nutrient-management goal. The testbed is complete, dataset collection is in progress, and model development has not yet begun.

## Study scope

- Crop: fenugreek.
- Growing method: aeroponics.
- Treatments: omission of selected fertilizers from the nutrient solution.
- Image collection: observations over time; capture schedule and imaging details are not yet documented here.

We vary the presence or absence of calcium nitrate, potassium sulphate, and ammonium phosphate. Our experiment studies combinations of these fertilizer compounds; it does not isolate individual nitrogen, phosphorus, or potassium elements. See [Fertilizer Treatments](#/docs/fertilizer-treatments) for the treatment codes.
